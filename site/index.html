FAQ

Are the shirts original?

Yes. Every shirt is official, and we check the product code on the inside tag against the season before it goes on sale.

How does grading work?

BNWT: brand new with tags. 10/10: no tags, but looks unworn. 9/10: worn a few times, no marks. 8/10: worn, still very good, any small mark is shown in the photos.

When will my order ship?

Your order ships once the PayPal payment is confirmed, with tracking. Delivery time for your country is shown in your bag before you pay.

Can I return a shirt?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, in the same condition you received it.

How do I pay?

By PayPal only. You can use your PayPal balance or a card through PayPal.

How do I sell to you?

Go to Sell To Us, fill in the form for up to 5 items with photos, and we reply with an offer within 24–48 hours. We pay sellers by PayPal.

If you live in the EU, you can cancel your purchase within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. Your legal rights under Decree-Law 84/2021 apply. Because most items are second-hand, the guarantee period for those items is 18 months from delivery. If an item is not as described, contact us and we will repair, replace, reduce the price or refund, as the law provides.

Offers are made within 24–48 hours after we receive your form and are based on the details and photos you send. The final price is confirmed once we receive and check the item.
